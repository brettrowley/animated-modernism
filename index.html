Modernism in Motion

The geometric paintings of modernists like László Moholy-Nagy, Kazimir Malevich and Josef Albers lend themselves naturally to digital design tools.

Claude learned to recreate and animate a collection of paintings, each in a style further from the last. Under the direction of Brett Rowley, corrections and adjustments to the animations were recorded in a Rulebook, a document that provides definitions, instructions and examples to guide the next animation.

The initial painting took over a dozen rounds of iteration to finish, despite its relatively simple construction. With the help of an expanded Rulebook, the final and most complex painting needed only 3 minor adjustments.
